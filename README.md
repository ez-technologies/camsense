# camsense

Camera recognition for the browser: point a phone at something and get back what matters, quickly.
Read the text on a label (lines and words, from the middle out), read a barcode, or take the sharpest
photo of a product in under a second. Everything runs on the phone; nothing is downloaded on devices
without a touch screen and a camera.

It grows out of [number-scanner-poc](https://github.com/israeleis/number-scanner-poc), where reading
serial labels was tested on a real phone. Its findings are in that repository's
[docs/INTEGRATION.md](https://github.com/israeleis/number-scanner-poc/blob/main/docs/INTEGRATION.md).

**Status:** design only. See [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md).
